# Asset Provenance

The imagery in this repository was created or generated for the **Shop with Confidence** personal hackathon prototype. OpenAI, ChatGPT, and Codex-assisted image-generation tooling was used during development.

Some generated files retain OpenAI C2PA provenance metadata. Other files may not retain generation metadata because they were converted or exported during development. This statement records the project's development provenance; it does not make additional legal or licensing claims beyond what can be established from the files and project records.

The assets are used only as fictional/demo retail content. Urban Threads, Vela, and other demo retail naming do not imply affiliation with a real retailer, and no third-party retailer endorsement is intended or implied.

YouCam and Perfect Corp, and Gemini and Google, are references to technologies used or discussed by the project; they are not fictional demo brands.
